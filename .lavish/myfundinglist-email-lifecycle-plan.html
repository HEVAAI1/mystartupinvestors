MFLMYFUNDINGLIST

Transactional & product-triggered email system

Every important account moment, explained clearly.

A verified sender and shared template are ready. This plan adds reliable delivery for billing, credits, founder workflows, support, and affiliates—without marketing campaigns.

Recommended release
15 customer emails · 2 internal notifications

Principle 01

Only after a durable event

An email is queued only after the purchase, credit change, or status change is saved successfully.

Principle 02

One message per moment

Unique event keys prevent webhook retries, page refreshes, and repeat requests from creating duplicates.

Principle 03

Useful, never promotional

No newsletters, campaigns, or re-engagement mail. Every email maps to an account action or a material product state.

Delivery architecture

A durable queue between product events and Resend

flowchart LR
  A[Verified product event] --> B[Database mutation + unique outbox event]
  B --> C[Email dispatcher]
  C --> D[Shared MyFundingList template]
  D --> E[Resend]
  E --> F[Delivery status + retry record]
  C --> G{Transient failure?}
  G -->|yes| H[Retry by scheduled job]
  G -->|no| F

The current Dodo webhook already prevents duplicate transactions. The outbox adds the same guarantee for receipts and all other emails.

Internal notifications

Two messages for the team

  • New contact request: keep the existing support alert, now in the shared template.
  • New startup submitted: notify the review team with a secure dashboard link, not sensitive profile data in the email.

Explicitly excluded

What we will not send

  • No newsletter, product promotion, or inactivity/re-engagement sequence.
  • No mail for every investor unlock, calculator use, duplicate webhook, or failed form validation.
  • No refund email until the product gains a verified refund event or admin flow.

Operations · Vercel-ready

No additional platform is required.

Vercel

Runs the app, protected email-dispatch endpoint, and a scheduled retry job for unsent outbox events.

Supabase

Stores each unique email event, delivery state, retry count, and the business records that trigger it.

Resend

Uses your verified sender to deliver the shared branded template and returns a delivery ID for auditability.

One deployment prerequisite: add the same Resend sender/API-key variables to Vercel, plus a protected cron secret. The app code, database migration, and schedule configuration ship together; there is no separate worker to run or maintain.

Decision required

Approve the transactional email set?

Approval means we will build the durable outbox, dispatcher, templates, and the 15 customer emails above. Refund mail remains intentionally out of scope until there is a reliable refund event.
